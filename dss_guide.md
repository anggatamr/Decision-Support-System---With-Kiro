# 🚀 DSS Dashboard: The Ultimate Gen Z Guide to Making Choices Without Stressing Out

Yo! Welcome to the **Decision Support System (DSS) Dashboard**. If you're tired of overthinking and just want hard data to tell you what to do, you're in the right place. We gave this whole thing a Neobrutalist glow-up so it looks fresh while doing the heavy lifting for your brain.

## What is this?
It's an app that helps you make decisions when things are certain, risky, or completely chaotic (uncertainty). It uses actual math to back up your choices so you don't have to guess.

## How to vibe with it:
### 1. Data-Driven DSS 📊
Got a dataset? Dump it here. It'll show you trends, correlations, and everything you need to know without writing code.
- **Try it:** Upload `example_dataset.csv` to see how much your startup is bleeding cash vs making profit.

### 2. Payoff Table 📋
The core of everything. You list your choices (Alternatives) and what might happen (Conditions/States).
- **Example:** "Should I launch a startup, get a 9-to-5, or become an influencer?"
- **Try it:** Open the Payoff Table, set 4 Alternatives and 3 Conditions, then type in the numbers from `example_payoff.csv`.

### 3. EV & EOL (Risk) 🎲
If you know the chances of things happening (e.g., 60% chance of going viral), use this. It tells you the Expected Value (what you'll likely get) and Opportunity Loss (what you missed out on).

### 4. Uncertainty ❓
When you have zero clue what's going to happen. It uses criteria like:
- **Maximax:** For the absolute optimists (YOLO).
- **Maximin:** For the pessimists (playing it safe).
- **Laplace:** Assuming everything has an equal chance.

### 5. Distribution 📈
Estimate probability distributions (Normal, Binomial, Poisson, Exponential, Uniform, Beta) directly from your uploaded data or manually input parameters. It shows you the PDF/PMF and the stats.

### 6. Utility ⚖️
Map out your own Utility Function (Risk Preference). You input a few data points (Money vs Utility), and it fits a curve to tell you if you're Risk Averse, Neutral, or Seeking.

### 7. Monte Carlo 🎰
Run a Monte Carlo simulation based on your Data-Driven results to predict future outcomes using random sampling.

### 8. Recommendation Engine 🏆
Too lazy to check all the tabs? Just click here. It takes all the methods you ran (Certainty, Risk, Uncertainty, Utility) and gives you the absolute best choice based on consensus.

## Example Files included:
1. `example_dataset.csv` - A fake dataset of Gen Z startup metrics (engagement, revenue, memes posted). Use it in **Data-Driven DSS**.
2. `example_payoff.csv` - A matrix showing payoff values for different career choices under different market conditions.
